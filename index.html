About Me

Hi, Visitor.

I am an Embedded Software Engineer with a strong focus on Linux systems and low-level software development. As a Linux kernel maintainer, I enjoy working close to the hardware, solving practical problems, and contributing to software that powers millions of devices.

Professional Journey

My work sits at the intersection of reliability, performance, and long-term maintainability. I am especially interested in embedded platforms, kernel internals, and building robust systems that can be trusted in real-world environments.

Academic and Learning Journey

I consider myself a forever learner. I continuously explore new tools, read technical papers and documentation, and refine my understanding of systems engineering one concept at a time. For me, growth is not a one-time milestone; it is a lifelong process.

Personal Side

Outside of engineering, I am a recreational tennis player and someone who values the little joys of life. I enjoy planting trees and plants, spending time outdoors, and staying grounded through simple, meaningful routines.

This website is where I share my projects, technical notes, and ideas from both my professional and personal journey.
